Editing Core

Timeline Architecture

One of the most commonly associated capabilities is timeline-based editing. This structure allows users to organize clips in a clear visual order, making it easier to build stories, control pacing, and fine-tune transitions. Timelines also support layered editing, where visuals, text, and sound can exist on separate tracks. This gives creators more control over how each element appears and interacts.

Visual customization is another core area people focus on. Mobile editors are expected to provide tools for adjusting brightness, contrast, color tone, and sharpness. These adjustments help match different scenes, correct lighting issues, and build a consistent look across an entire video. Beyond manual controls, built-in visual presets and motion effects allow creators to quickly add style and atmosphere.
